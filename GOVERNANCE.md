# Governance

Daedalus is a maintainer-led project. The current maintainer and decision owner is [@sonan0721](https://github.com/sonan0721). Contributors can propose changes and challenge decisions; the maintainer is responsible for resolving project direction and accepting changes.

## Decisions

Use issues to clarify goals, constraints, and open questions. Record significant architecture or policy choices in an architecture decision record (ADR) with the context, decision, tradeoffs, and conditions that could justify revisiting it. Keep proposals open for feedback before treating them as settled.

## Reviews and merges

There is no team structure or fixed minimum number of approvals today. The maintainer may merge a focused pull request they authored once CI passes and the change is ready. Additional human review is welcome when available; AI agents do not count as independent human reviewers and cannot make project decisions for the maintainer.

The repository is in a documentation and research stage and is intended to contain engine work only. Small fixtures or test scenes may be added when they support a specific engine test. Sample game projects, demos, and cloned games are outside the current scope.

For contribution workflow and current checks, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [TESTING.md](TESTING.md). Security reports are covered in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
