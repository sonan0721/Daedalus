# Code of conduct

We want Daedalus discussions to be useful, welcoming, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat others with respect, discuss ideas without attacking people, and make room for questions and corrections.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, and sharing someone's private information without consent. Keep feedback specific and focused on the work.

The maintainer is responsible for responding to conduct concerns and may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe. To report a concern, contact [@sonan0721](https://github.com/sonan0721) privately through GitHub. If a private route is unavailable, open a brief issue asking for a private contact route and leave out names and sensitive details.
